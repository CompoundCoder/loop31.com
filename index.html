How it works

Nobody needs a house or a car every week. When they do, you want to be the first person they think of. Loop31 keeps you there with a few friendly posts a week.

43% of home buyers found their agent through a friend, neighbor, or relative.National Association of Realtors, 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, as summarized by BAM

Ready-made Loops for your line of work

Loop31 starts you with 30 post ideas for real estate or car sales, in six Loops: tips, reviews from people you've helped, your town, a look behind the scenes, and more. Fill in the blanks, and each post comes back around on its own, at least eight weeks apart. You can make your own Loops too.

A minute a week

Once a week, next week's posts are ready for a look, one at a time, the way they'll appear. Tap Looks Good, Skip, or Change, and your week is set.

A second look before your name goes on it

Every caption is checked against the advertising rules for your line of work. The app points the words out. You decide.

Sunny two-bedroom near the park, perfect for a young family. One phrase to look at: it says who the home is for.

Share in about 20 seconds

Your personal Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok is where friends and family see you, and no app can post there for you. So Loop31 gets each post ready: the picture is made, the caption is copied, and your iPhone reminds you when it's time. Tap Share, paste, and post.

If you say yes, an outside AI service can write fresh versions of a caption, so a post that comes back around doesn't read the same. You see every version first, and the app works fully without it.

The hook goes on the picture, and the caption lands it in the first line. The app adds your name, brokerage, and phone under every post.

For brokerages and dealerships

Give everyone on your team Loop31, so each of them stays top of mind with the people they know.

A manager invites each person, gives them a seat, and sees the posts they share from the day they join. Everyone posts from their own accounts, and managers never see drafts, photos, or anything that wasn't posted.
