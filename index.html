How it works

A minute a week.

No writing, no design, no scheduling app to learn. Your personal Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok is where friends and family see you, and no app can post there for you. So Loop31 gets each post ready, and you share it.

  1. 1

    Next week is ready

    Once a week, Loop31 picks a few posts from your Loops and fills them in with your details.

  2. 2

    Look it over in a minute

    One post at a time, the way it will look. Tap Looks Good, Skip, or Edit. Nothing posts unseen.

  3. 3

    Share in about 20 seconds

    Your iPhone reminds you when it’s time. The picture is made and the caption is copied. Tap Share, paste, and post.

The small things that make it yours.

A second look before your name goes on it

Every caption is checked against the advertising rules for your line of work. The app points the words out. You decide.

Sunny two-bedroom near the park, perfect for a young family. One phrase to look at: it says who the home is for.

A reminder when it’s time

Another version, if you want one

If you say yes, an outside AI service can write fresh versions of a caption, so a post that comes back around doesn’t read the same. You see every version first, and the app works fully without it.

Your name on every post

The app adds your name, brokerage or dealership, and phone under every post and along the bottom of every picture.
