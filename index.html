Joe Girard sent every customer a card, every month.

13,001new cars and trucks Girard sold at one Chevrolet store in Eastpointe, Michigan, from 1963 to 1977.1

Every customer, more than 13,000 of them, got a piece of mail from him once a month, with a different picture each time and the words “I like you.”2,3 When they needed a car again, his was the name they knew.

Your best people already stay in touch like that. Most don’t, because coming up with something to post every week, for years, is the hard part. Loop31 does that part for every salesperson, with your store’s name on every post.

The card was one part of how Girard sold. He also paid people for referrals and worked them hard.2

Most customers mean to come back. Few do.

  • 91%of home buyers would use their agent again or recommend them, in the National Association of Realtors’ 2025 survey of buyers and sellers.4
  • 18%of the repeat buyers in that survey went back to the agent they had used before.5
  • 37.9%of returning car buyers bought from the same dealer at Subaru, which won S&P Global Mobility’s 2025 award for loyalty to the dealer. Even at the best brand, most went somewhere else.6

Not all of that gap is forgetting: people move, and salespeople change jobs.

Loop31 does the hard part.

Facebook and Instagram don’t let an app post to a personal account, so Loop31 gets each post ready and the salesperson shares it from their own. That’s where their friends see it.

    Each person gets 4 ready posts a week, or up to one a day: tips, customer reviews, their part of town. Every one carries your store’s name.

  2. 2

    Looks good or Skip on each post, or Edit words, designed to take about a minute a week. Nothing goes out that they haven’t seen.

  3. 3

    Their iPhone reminds them when it’s time. The caption is already copied, so sharing takes about 20 seconds.

Every post is checked before it’s shared, and you keep the record.

  • Your name on every post Your store’s or brokerage’s name goes on the picture and under the caption of every post, and it can’t be turned off. As the owner you can add one office logo and one office line, such as “Each Office Independently Owned and Operated”.
  • No prices or payments from us The posts Loop31 writes never quote a price or a payment. If a salesperson adds a monthly payment or a down payment to a car post, Loop31 stops it until the full credit terms are there or the payment comes out.
  • Fair housing wording When a real estate caption says who a home is for, like “perfect for a young family”, the words are marked in orange with a suggested change. The agent decides.
  • The check record For each post that goes out, the portal keeps which checks ran and what they flagged, never the words. Export it for any dates as a spreadsheet.
  • Nothing flagged the checks found nothing
  • A warning wording worth a second look
  • A rule something the post needed and didn’t have

These checks catch common problems. They aren’t legal advice, and the record never says a post “passed”. The rules behind them are on the dealership and brokerage pages.

Questions managers ask

Who should approve it?
At a dealership, the general manager or dealer principal, since every post carries the store’s name. At a brokerage, the broker-owner or managing broker; in Indiana, all advertising is under the managing broker’s supervision. A team lead can start it for their own agent team; tell your managing broker first. More on the dealership and brokerage pages.
Will my people actually post?
The posts are ready, checking them is designed to take about a minute a week, and their phone reminds them when it’s time. You see who posted, and you can send a reminder to someone whose post is due today. Seat only the people who want it, and try it free for 30 days with your own team.
Do you need their Facebook or Instagram passwords?
No, never. Each person shares from their own phone, and Loop31 never posts for anyone.
Do you get our customer list?
No. Loop31 never asks for, imports or reads your customers’ names, numbers or deals, and it doesn’t connect to your CRM or DMS. For a dealership that connects its website, it reads only the cars the store publishes. More on the security and data page.
What happens when someone leaves?
Take their seat back in the portal and give it to the next hire. Their Facebook and Instagram friends stay theirs, as they would anyway; while they hold your seat, every post carries your name.
Won’t everyone’s posts look the same?
Each person starts the post ideas at a different place. When two people on your team are about to share nearly the same post, the app says so and offers a fresh version.
Is there a contract?
No annual contract. Seats are month to month, and you can cancel anytime.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet. Loop31 is iPhone only for now.
