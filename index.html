Keep your social media going without thinking about it every day.

Loop31 is an iPhone app for real estate agents and car salespeople. You set it up once with the kinds of posts you want to share. From then on, your posts repeat on their own, you look them over for about a minute each week, and every caption gets a second look at its wording before it goes out.

How it works

  • Posts that repeat on their own Home tips, client reviews, your neighborhood, behind the scenes: each is a Loop of posts that come back around, at least eight weeks apart, so your accounts never go quiet.
  • A one-minute look over each week Once a week the app shows you next week's posts, one at a time, the way they will look. Tap Looks Good, Skip, or Change. Nothing posts unseen.
  • A second look at wording before it posts Every caption is checked against the advertising rules for your line of work: payment terms in a car ad, or wording that says who a home is for in a listing. The app points the words out. You decide.

Posting takes a few taps

Most people post from their personal Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok, and no app can post to those for you. So Loop31 gets each post completely ready: the photo is sized, the caption is copied, and one tap hands it to the app you post from. You paste and post, in about 20 seconds.

If you say yes, an outside AI service can write fresh versions of your captions so repeats don't sound the same, and, for real estate, point out wording to double-check. You see every version before it goes anywhere, and the app works fully without it.
