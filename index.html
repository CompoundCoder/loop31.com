Loop31 writes each post and makes its picture. You look it over and share it.

Facebook and Instagram don’t let an app post to your personal account, so Loop31 gets each post ready and you tap Share.

What goes out under your name.

  • The headline goes big on the picture, and the caption picks it up in its first line.
  • Your name and number are on the picture and under the caption, on every post.
  • Before you see the caption, it’s checked against the advertising rules for your line of work. Anything worth a second look is marked in orange, and you decide.
  • Your iPhone reminds you when it’s time to post. Loop31nowTime to post: Find your water shutoffTap Post now to copy the caption.
  • Change the words before it goes out: the caption and the words on the picture.

Run an office or a sales floor?

Give each agent or salesperson a seat for $10 a month. Everyone posts from their own accounts. You see who’s posting, and you never see drafts, photos, or anything that wasn’t posted.
