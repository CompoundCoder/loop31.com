How it works

You set it up once with the kinds of posts you want to share. Three things keep it going from there.

Posts that repeat on their own

Home tips, client reviews, your neighborhood, behind the scenes. Each is a Loop of posts that come back around, at least eight weeks apart, so your accounts never go quiet.

Photo

A one-minute look each week

Once a week the app shows you next week's posts, one at a time, the way they will look. Nothing posts unseen.

Looks Good Skip Change

A second look at wording

Every caption is checked against the advertising rules for your line of work. The app points the words out. You decide.

Sunny two-bedroom near the park, perfect for a young family. One phrase to look at: it says who the home is for.

Posting takes a few taps

Most people post from their personal Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok, and no app can post to those for you. So Loop31 gets each post completely ready: the photo is sized, the caption is copied, and one tap hands it to the app you post from. You paste and post, in about 20 seconds.

If you say yes, an outside AI service can write fresh versions of your captions so repeats don't sound the same. You see every version before it goes anywhere, and the app works fully without it.

What a post looks like

Every post starts from an idea in your starter pack. This one is from the Home Tips Loop for real estate, with the blank filled in.

The hook goes on the picture. The caption lands it in the first line. The app adds your name, brokerage, and phone under every post.
