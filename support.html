Support

Write to support@loop31.com. A person reads every message. During the beta we answer within a business day, usually sooner.

What to include

The more of this you can send, the faster we can help:

  • the email address you sign in with;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About);
  • what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • a screenshot, if there is something to see. Please cover anything private first.

Never send a sign-in code. We will never ask for one.

Common questions

What is Loop31?
An iPhone app that keeps real estate agents and car salespeople top of mind with friends, family, and followers. It starts you with 30 friendly post ideas for your line of work. Fill in an idea's blanks once, and that post comes back around on its own: Loop31 plans a few posts a week and gets each one ready for you to share from your own accounts.
How do I sign in? I don't have a password.
There are no passwords. Type your email and the app sends a 6-digit code to it. Enter the code and you are in. The same steps create your account the first time and sign you in after that.
The code didn't come.
Give it a minute and check your junk or spam folder. A code works for 10 minutes. If it has expired or still hasn't arrived, tap New code. If you typed the wrong address, tap Change email. If none of that works, email us from the address you used.
How does Check posts work?
Once a week (Sunday evening, unless you pick another day in Me), Check posts shows you next week's posts one at a time, the way they will look. For each one tap Looks good or Skip, or Edit words to change the caption or the words on the picture. It takes about a minute. Nothing posts unseen: a post you have not looked at does not go out. You can stop partway and finish later from Home or Week.
What does "a second look at wording" mean?
Every caption is checked against the advertising rules for your line of work. If something might raise a question, the app highlights those words in orange and often suggests a change. It is a pointer, not a ruling: you read it and decide. The app does not give legal advice.
Does Loop31 post for me?
Not yet. Today you share every post yourself: the app gets it fully ready, you tap Share, the caption is already copied, and you paste and post in the app you use. Posting on your behalf, for professional accounts that allow it, is planned for later. Loop31 never posts to a personal account and never asks for its password.
What does the outside AI service see, if I turn on writing help?
Writing help is how the app gets fresh words for a caption. For real estate and car sales, that's Mix It Up, which the app offers when a teammate posted something too similar. If your line of work is Other, it's also New version, on captions you write yourself. The service sees only caption text: the caption, the words in it that must stay exact, your line of work, and the tone you picked. Never your name, phone, email, photos, client reviews, or anything about your account. The privacy policy names the companies and lists exactly what goes to them. You can leave writing help off and the app works fully without it.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Me and tap Delete account. The screen lists what will be removed. Everything we hold for you goes: your profile, Loops, posts, photos, schedule, and history. Pictures the app saved to your Photos stay, because the app can only add to Photos, not delete from it; remove them yourself if you like. If you can't open the app, email support@loop31.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete the account for you.
Can my whole office or dealership use it?
Yes, with Teams. A managing broker or sales manager invites each person with a 6-digit code and chooses whose seats the office pays for. To join, open Me, tap Plan, then Join a team, and enter the code. The manager sees which Loops you have on and the posts you share from the day you join, and can send you one reminder a day when a post is due; never your drafts, photos, or anything you haven't posted. To set up a team, see pricing and seats or start a free 30-day pilot.
Can I turn off the reminders?
Yes. Reminders are ordinary iPhone notifications, so you can turn them off in Settings → Notifications → Loop31, or in the app. The app still works; you will just need to open it yourself.
