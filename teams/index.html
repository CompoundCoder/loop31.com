How seats work

You invite each person by email with a 6-digit code. They join in the app, after it shows them exactly what you’ll see.

You choose who holds a seat. Take a seat back and give it to someone else whenever you like.

As the owner, you can set one office logo and one office line, such as “Each Office Independently Owned and Operated”. Every seated person’s posts carry the line. The logo goes on the posts of anyone without a logo of their own, or on everyone’s if you require it.

Each person gets a few ready posts a week, looks them over in the app and shares them from their own accounts. Their iPhone reminds them when it’s time.

What you see, and what you never see

You see

  • each person’s name, and that their seat is paid
  • when they joined, and the date of their last post
  • which Loops they have on (you can’t change them)
  • the posts that went out from the day they joined, with dates and captions (captions for 30 days)
  • a record of what the checks flagged on each shared post
  • a Monday email with last week’s numbers, never captions or photos

You never see

  • drafts, or posts they skipped
  • their photos
  • listings or vehicles they’ve added
  • their phone number or setup answers
  • anyone who pays for Loop31 on their own
