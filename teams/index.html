How seats work

You invite each person by email with a 6-digit code. They join in the app, after it shows them exactly what you’ll see.

You choose who holds a seat. Take a seat back and give it to someone else whenever you like, such as a new hire on their first day.

The team’s owner sets the seats, the office logo and the office line, and can make others admins. Owners and admins see the team, send reminders and export the record.

Each person gets their posts, looks them over in the app and shares them from their own accounts. Their iPhone reminds them when it’s time.
