Your brokerage name on every post.

  • The brokerage’s name In Indiana, an internet ad that carries a licensee’s name must also carry the broker company’s name (876 IAC 8-1-8).4 NAR’s Standard of Practice 12-5 asks for the firm name too, and a team name alone isn’t enough.5 Loop31 puts your brokerage’s name on the picture and under the caption of every post, and it can’t be turned off. You can add your office’s logo and one office line too.
  • Wording about who a home is for Indiana’s Civil Rights Commission counts social media posts as housing ads.6 When a caption says who a home is for, like “perfect for a young family”, the app marks those words in orange and suggests a change, with the Fair Housing Act’s advertising rule (42 U.S.C. 3604(c)) in mind.7 The agent decides.
  • Equal Housing Opportunity The logo goes on listing posts by default, and an agent can choose to put it on every post.

What you see as their broker.

You see

  • each agent’s name, and that their seat is paid
  • when they joined, and the date of their last post
  • which Loops they have on (you can’t change them)
  • the posts that went out from the day they joined, with dates and captions (captions for 30 days)
  • a record of what the checks flagged on each shared post
  • a Monday email with last week’s numbers, never captions or photos

You never see

  • drafts, or posts they skipped
  • their photos or their listings
  • their phone number or setup answers
  • agents who pay for Loop31 on their own

Questions brokers ask

Will my agents actually use it?
It’s designed to take about a minute a week, and their phone reminds them when it’s time to post. You can see who posted. Seat only the agents who want it, and try it free for 30 days with your own office.
We already have a CRM and a marketing center.
Loop31 isn’t a CRM or a design tool. It hands each agent a week of ready, checked posts for their own profile, which is the part agents usually pay for themselves. It sits next to the tools you already have.
Our franchise has rules for agents’ posts.
The posts go on the agent’s own profile, not your office’s page, and your brokerage’s name is on every one. As the owner, you set one office logo and one office line, such as “Each Office Independently Owned and Operated”, and every seated agent’s posts carry the line. The logo goes on the posts of agents without a logo of their own, or on everyone’s if you require it.
Will agents mind me seeing their posts?
You see only what they post, from the day they join, and the app tells them exactly that before they join.
Which states do the checks cover?
They’re written for Indiana’s rules first. If your office is in another state, ask us before you start.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet. Loop31 is iPhone only for now.
