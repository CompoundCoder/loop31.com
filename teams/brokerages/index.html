Your brokerage name on every post, and a record of the checks.

  • The brokerage’s name In Indiana, an internet ad that carries a licensee’s name must also carry the broker company’s name (876 IAC 8-1-8).5 NAR’s Standard of Practice 12-5 asks for the firm name too, and a team name alone isn’t enough.6 Loop31 puts your brokerage’s name on the picture and under the caption of every post, and it can’t be turned off.
  • Wording about who a home is for Indiana’s Civil Rights Commission counts social media posts as housing ads.7 When a caption says who a home is for, like “perfect for a young family”, the app marks those words in orange and suggests a change, with the Fair Housing Act’s advertising rule (42 U.S.C. 3604(c)) in mind.8 The agent decides.
  • Equal Housing Opportunity The logo goes on listing posts by default, and an agent can choose to put it on every post.
  • The check record For each post that goes out, the portal keeps which checks ran and what they flagged: “nothing flagged”, “a warning” or “a rule”, never the words. Export it for any dates as a spreadsheet. It helps you document supervision.

Questions brokers ask

Will my agents actually use it?
It’s designed to take about a minute a week, and their phone reminds them when it’s time to post. You see who posted, and you can send a reminder to an agent whose post is due today. Seat only the agents who want it, and try it free for 30 days with your own office.
We already have a CRM and a marketing center.
Loop31 isn’t a CRM or a design tool. It hands each agent a week of ready, checked posts for their own profile, which is the part agents usually pay for themselves. It sits next to the tools you already have.
Our franchise has rules for agents’ posts.
The posts go on the agent’s own profile, not your office’s page, and your brokerage’s name is on every one. Your office line, such as “Each Office Independently Owned and Operated”, goes on every seated agent’s posts.
Will agents mind me seeing their posts?
You see only what they post, from the day they join, and the app tells them exactly that before they join.
Do you get our clients’ information?
No. Loop31 doesn’t connect to your CRM and never reads a client list. The only client words in it are reviews an agent types in with the client’s OK. More on the security and data page.
Which states do the checks cover?
They’re written for Indiana’s rules first. If your office is in another state, ask us before you start.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet. Loop31 is iPhone only for now.
